Hi, I’m Jithendaraa.

Email Google Scholar GitHub Twitter

At Toyota Research Institute, I pretrain multimodal foundation models for materials science and post-train LLMs to accelerate materials discovery.

I’ve trained models to identify crystalline phases from X-ray diffraction patterns and perform Rietveld refinement.

My research interests span AI for science, AI safety, and RL for LLM post-training. I am passionate about building robust training infra and writing high performance code.

Update: Looking for full-time research engineer and research scientist roles in 2026!

Research background

I completed my M.Sc. at McGill University and Mila supervised by Derek Nowrouzezahrai and Samira Ebrahimi Kahou.

During my master’s, I worked on problems at the intersection of causal discovery and representation learning and GFlowNets.

My thesis was on learning a joint distribution over causal variables, structures, and mechanisms from low-level data. In 2024, I was a visiting researcher at ServiceNow Research, where I worked on change point detection in temporal causal models, and benchmarking text-conditioned forecasting capabilities of LLMs. I spent summer 2023 as a research scientist intern at Amazon training large-scale models for long-term revenue forecasting.

Before my graduate studies, I completed my undergrad from NIT Trichy, India. As a research intern with the RoboTutor Team at Carnegie Mellon University, under Jack Mostow, I built intelligent tutoring systems for underprivileged students in Tanzania by training RL policies to personalize the sequence of exercises for each student. I also explored machine learning for security during a research internship at UC Berkeley with Dawn Song and Joseph Near.
